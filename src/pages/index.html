Who we are

There's no such thing as an audience.

There are only people, and people are moved by the same things: something real, said by someone who means it. We're a video production company in Phoenix, and we make nonfiction films with real people telling their own stories, with no actors, no narration, no stock footage and nothing telling you what to feel. Anyone can buy a cinema camera. The hard part is everything that comes before it: the research, the hard questions and the patience to wait for an honest answer. That's the part we're known for. How we work

We call it the Thrill Wave treatment, and three things stay the same on every project, from an aftermovie to a docuseries:

  1. 01

    Do the hard part first

    Before a camera turns on, we know the world, we know who we're filming and we've earned their trust.

  2. 02

    Argue for the good version

    Every project has a safe version and a good version, and we'll push for the good one and tell you why.

  3. 03

    Put our name on it

    We take every film as seriously as the person it's for does, and we don't put our name on anything we don't mean.
