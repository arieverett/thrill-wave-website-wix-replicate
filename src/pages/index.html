Who we are

There's no such thing as an audience.

There are only people, and people take in a story in remarkably similar ways. Nobody has lived on the desert planet in Dune, but readers everywhere have felt its grief, fear and loyalty for sixty years, because those are true for everyone. So we don't make films for demographics. We make them for what's true about being human, and that kind of film keeps working long after the trend it could have chased is gone. We're a video production company in Phoenix, and the camera is the easy part. The hard part is finding that truth in your story. Here's what that looks like:

How we work

We call it the Thrill Wave treatment, and three things stay the same on every project, from an aftermovie to a docuseries:

  1. 01

    Find the person first

    Before a camera turns on, we find out whose life the work changes and what it means to them.

  2. 02

    Argue for the good version

    Every project has a safe version and a good version, and we'll push for the good one and tell you why.

  3. 03

    Put our name on it

    We take every film as seriously as the person it's for does, and we don't put our name on anything we don't mean.
