About Thrill Wave · Est. 2018

Made of people, for people.

Thrill Wave is a video production company in Phoenix, Arizona. Every film we make starts with one person: we find out whose life the work changes, put them on screen and let them speak for themselves.

Origin

Founded in 2018 in Phoenix, Arizona.

We started Thrill Wave because we love making things with people, and we kept watching the people get lost. Dated processes, smoke-and-mirrors pricing and endless bidding took the heart out of something that should be simple: good people, a true story and the care to tell it well. So we built the company we would want to hire, and we've tried to do right by everyone who walks through the door ever since. Here's where that has taken us so far:

  • 2018Founded in Phoenix
  • 10+Years behind the camera
  • 80+Organizations we've worked with
  • 12Fields and counting

Our founders came to film from very different places, and each path taught us something we still bring to set, from the rhythm of a good edit to the habit of asking why a story matters. Thrill Wave is where it all comes together.

Our manifesto

What we believe.

Here's what we believe about making films, and why it shapes every one we make:

There's no such thing as an audience. There are only people. Every brief we've read describes a target audience, and no one in a target audience has ever been moved as one. People are moved one at a time, by someone they recognize. So we make every film for one person, and the crowd recognizes itself.

Every organization changes someone's life. A tribal council, a bank, a festival, a clinic: somewhere, the work lands on a person. Before we film, we find out whose life it is and what it means to them. Then we put them on screen and let them speak for themselves.

Nobody watches homework. Most films that institutions make set out to explain something, and most go unwatched. When a brief asks us to teach, we look for the person the lesson happened to. A series on how government works became ten episodes about the people government touches, and our client trusted us to make it that way.

We take a side, and we tell you whose. There's no such thing as a neutral film. Every cut is a choice about what stays and what goes, and a film that claims no point of view is hiding its author. We'd rather show you ours.

We'll argue for the good version. Every project has a safe version and a good version. The safe one does what was asked. The good one does what was needed. We'll push for the good one every time, and we'll tell you why. If you want exactly what you asked for, we're probably the wrong crew.

The best night of someone's year counts too. Some people wait all year for one night at a festival. When we cut the aftermovie, we cut it for them, with the same care we give our most serious documentary, because to them it matters just as much.

Our name goes on it. You don't hire a few people with cameras. You get the Thrill Wave treatment: people who care what happens after the film goes out. We don't put our name on anything we don't mean.

Under all of it sits one promise: we protect the people we film. We ask for consent first and treat everyone with dignity, especially when the story is personal.

Our values

What we care about.

Those beliefs show up as a few habits, from what we shoot to what we cut and when we speak up. They're what you can count on from us on every project:

  1. 01

    We keep it true

    We report first, check our facts and let people speak for themselves. Everyone on screen is a real person with something real at stake. We don't stage what should be felt, and we don't promise what we can't film.

  2. 02

    We keep it simple

    Untangling a complicated subject is our job, not the viewer's, so the message reaches people clearly.

  3. 03

    We care about the craft

    We love the problem-solving on set, the light and the edit that makes it all click. If a cut doesn't move us, we don't expect it to move anyone else, so we keep working until it does.

  4. 04

    We stay accountable

    You'll know who's on your project before the first shoot day, and we answer for how it turns out.

  5. 05

    We talk straight

    We're honest about scope, cost and timeline from the start, so there are no surprises, upsells or overpromises later.

  6. 06

    We care what happens next

    The final cut isn't the finish line. We pay attention to where the film lands and what it does for the people who watch it.
